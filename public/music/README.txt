Drop your audio files here (mp3, m4a, ogg, wav, flac, opus, aac) and rebuild / restart the dev server.
They show up in the notch music player automatically, sorted by file name.

Naming:  "Artist - Title.mp3"   ->  artist + title are shown separately
         "Title.mp3"            ->  just the title
         "01 - Artist - Title.mp3" -> a leading number only controls the order

Tip: 96-128 kbps keeps each song around 3-4 MB. Nothing is downloaded until someone presses play.
